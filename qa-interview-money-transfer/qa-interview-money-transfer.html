Your assignment

You are testing the money transfer feature of the VASUP Bank app. Use exploratory testing to design test scenarios, then talk the interviewer through them out loud — the workspace below is there if you want to jot anything down, but writing is optional.

  1. Explore both transfer methods, amount limits, and the identity-verification rule.
  2. Design scenarios that cover happy-path, negative, and edge cases — as many as you can within the time.
  3. Talk the interviewer through each scenario out loud: what you'd do, the test data, the expected result, and how you'd prioritize it by risk.
  4. Be ready to explain what you would automate, what you would not, and why.
