Your assignment

You are testing keycard-controlled access to a single elevator in VASUP Tower. Use exploratory testing, then record the highest-value functional and non-functional test cases in the workspace below.

  1. Explore keycard access levels, floor selection limits, and the elevator's safety features.
  2. Consider positive, negative, boundary, accessibility, security, and reliability scenarios.
  3. Write clear steps and expected results. Prioritize by occupant safety and business risk.
  4. Be ready to explain what you would automate, what you would not, and why.
